# T10: Help screen, Esc menu, first-game tips

Create `scripts/menu.gd` (class `Menu`, extends `CanvasLayer`) and wire it into `scripts/main.gd`. Same look as the
HUD (Kenney Future font, rounded panels). All built in code.

- **Help** (`H` key or a `?` button in the HUD's bottom-left): a centred panel titled **DON'T PANIC** in large,
  friendly letters (font size 96, warm yellow #ffd23f with a dark outline), then the rules in short lines:
  dig (left click), towel flag (right click), numbers count mines around a tile (0-6), click a number whose
  flags are all placed to open the rest, you can only dig next to your land or a flag, palms give wood, chests and
  wrecks give gold, the three tools and their prices, 3 crew, win by digging every safe tile. Close with `H`,
  `Esc` or a Close button. The game is paused while it is open (`get_tree().paused`; the Menu node uses
  `process_mode = PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`).
- **Esc menu**: Resume, Restart (new game, short intro later), Help, Music volume and SFX volume sliders (audio
  buses "Music" and "SFX"; create them in code with `AudioServer` if missing), Quit. Pauses the game.
- **Tips** (first game of a session only): three one-time toasts at the top centre for 5 s each: after the first
  reveal ("Numbers count the mines next to a tile."), the first time a number appears ("Click a number when all
  its mines have towels to open the rest."), the first tool use ("Tools cost wood: chop palms by digging them up.").

Markers: `HELP_OPEN`, `HELP_CLOSE`, `MENU_OPEN`, `MENU_CLOSE`, `TIP <n>`. With `--autopilot`, after `START`: open
help, wait 0.5 s, close it; open the Esc menu, wait 0.5 s, resume; then play as before (the autopilot never
restarts or quits through the menu).

**Files:** create `scripts/menu.gd`; edit `scripts/main.gd` and `scripts/hud.gd` (the `?` button only).

**Acceptance check:** `tools/play.sh 120 "START HELP_OPEN HELP_CLOSE MENU_OPEN MENU_CLOSE TIP END FINISH" --autopilot --skip-intro`
must end with `CHECK OK`, and `tools/test.sh tests/test_hud.gd` must still pass.
