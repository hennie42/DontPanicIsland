# T11: Intro: Commissioner Grumbleflox mines the island

Create `scripts/intro.gd` (class `Intro`, extends `Node3D`) and start it from `scripts/main.gd` before play.

```gdscript
signal finished
func play(full: bool, rig: CameraRig) -> void   # full: ~12 s; short: ~3 s; emits `finished`; skippable
```

The full intro (times approximate, all motion by `Tween`, deterministic):
1. 0-3 s: the camera starts far out (`rig.distance = 22`, slowly rotating). The UFO (`Art.prop("ufo")` scaled x2,
   with `Art.prop("alien")` sitting in its dome: a child at the dome's centre, y offset +0.35 in UFO space) wobbles
   in from off-screen at height 6, bobbing (sin) and tilting.
2. 3-8 s: it crosses the island; Grumbleflox drops 8 bombs (`Art.prop("bomb")`, x2) at **decoy** positions (random
   land cells, NOT the real mines; the real mines are only placed on the player's first click anyway). Each bomb
   falls for 0.6 s and vanishes in a puff (a short `GPUParticles3D` or `CPUParticles3D` burst of sand-coloured
   spheres) just above the ground. A sperm whale (`res://assets/generated/whale.glb`) and a bowl of petunias
   (`res://assets/generated/petunias.glb`) also fall out, tumbling, and disappear into the sea with a splash puff.
   A speech bubble (a `Label3D` billboard above the UFO, Kenney Future font, white panel look) says
   "Galactic Planning Office notice: this island is scheduled for demolition. Have a nice day."
3. 8-11 s: the UFO zips off; the camera swoops in to the play view (`rig.distance = 14`, yaw 0).
4. Emit `finished`.
The short intro: the UFO zips across once (3 s), drops 3 bombs, no whale, no bubble.

Any key or mouse click skips to the end (camera at the play view, intro nodes freed). First game of a session:
full; Restart / Play again: short; `--skip-intro`: none. Markers `INTRO_START full|short`, `INTRO_END t=<seconds>`.
The autopilot does NOT skip the intro (unless `--skip-intro`) and starts playing after `INTRO_END`.

**Files:** create `scripts/intro.gd`; edit `scripts/main.gd`.

**Acceptance check:** `tools/play.sh 150 "INTRO_START INTRO_END START END FINISH" --autopilot` must end with
`CHECK OK` and the log's `INTRO_END t=` must be between 9 and 14 seconds (the director checks the frames).
