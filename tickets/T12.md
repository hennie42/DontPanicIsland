# T12: Win and lose screens

Create `scripts/ending.gd` (class `Ending`, extends `Node`) and show it from `scripts/main.gd` when the game ends.

- **Lose**: every mine cell pops up: `Art.prop("bomb_red")` rises from each mine tile with a bounce (`Tween`,
  `TRANS_BOUNCE`, staggered 0.05 s apart). The UFO (`Art.prop("ufo")` x2 with the alien, as in the intro) swoops
  back over the island and a speech bubble says "Resistance is useless! Your island has been bypassed."
- **Win**: confetti (`CPUParticles3D`, bright colours) over the camp; a pile of coins (`Art.prop("coin_stack")`,
  10 of them stacked/scattered) at the first revealed cell; the UFO flees upwards; big text
  "So long, and thanks for all the treasure".
- Both: after 2 s a centred panel (HUD look) with the result, the score breakdown (gold, crew x 100, turn bonus),
  the best score (`Board.load_best()`), and buttons **Play again** (restart: a new board with a new random seed, the
  short intro) and **Quit**.

Markers: `ENDING win|lose`, `ENDING_PANEL`. The autopilot waits for `ENDING_PANEL`, then 1 s, then `FINISH` and
quits (instead of quitting right after `END`).

**Files:** create `scripts/ending.gd`; edit `scripts/main.gd`.

**Acceptance check:**
`tools/play.sh 150 "END ENDING ENDING_PANEL FINISH" --autopilot --skip-intro && tools/play.sh 90 "ENDING ENDING_PANEL FINISH" --autopilot --skip-intro --force-lose`
must end with `CHECK OK`.
